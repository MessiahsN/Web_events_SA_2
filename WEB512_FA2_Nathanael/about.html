About Us

A four-year-old Gqeberha organisation promoting the events, attractions and wildlife of the Eastern Cape.

Our story

Explore SA Events was founded in 2022 in Bethelsdorp, Gqeberha, by community members who wanted to share the province with the world. In four years we have grown from small weekend gatherings into a calendar of festivals, safaris and conservation days.

We are now growing to welcome international visitors from Europe, North America and Asia, with English-language support, dollar pricing and airport transfers.

Our mission

To make South Africa’s events easy to find, easy to book and good for the people and wildlife that host them.

Our goals

  • Reach international customers
  • Support local artists and guides
  • Fund conservation of endangered species

Did you know?

Elephants

Addo Elephant National Park was created to save the region’s elephants from extinction.

African penguins

Bird Island in Algoa Bay shelters one of the world’s largest breeding colonies of this endangered species.

Black rhino

Private reserves in the Eastern Cape run breeding and protection programmes for critically endangered black rhino.
